Perspective

I enjoy work that requires thought, curiosity, and creating something useful. Across roles and disciplines, I am often drawn to the same underlying work: organizing information, understanding how systems fit together, connecting ideas, and helping people make clearer decisions.

Whether the work involves an environmental program, a geospatial project, technical writing, a data process, or a cross-functional initiative, I find satisfaction in turning complexity into clarity and helping ideas become practical work. I tend to listen before I speak, ask questions before reaching conclusions, and reconsider an initial view as new information or another perspective emerges.

Places have shaped me, too. I grew up in Texas, and the Hill Country, spring thunderstorms, bluebonnet season, the Gulf Coast, and open landscapes that feel a little less hurried remain familiar parts of my perspective. Southern California is where I built my career, deepened my commitment to environmental stewardship and public service, and learned from remarkable colleagues while working on watersheds and sustainability. Both places continue to influence how I think.

Relationships matter to me, and those connections extend across Texas, California, and elsewhere. Away from work, I am usually drawn to books, coffee, travel, coastlines and wild places, and time with animals and plants—the quieter parts of life that keep me curious and observant.

What Energizes Me

  • Turning complexity into clarity
  • Organizing information
  • Understanding systems
  • Helping people make informed decisions
  • Turning ideas into something tangible
  • Building useful programs and processes
  • Connecting ideas across disciplines
  • Learning new tools and technologies
  • Environmental stewardship
  • Meaningful collaboration

If this perspective aligns with the needs or values of your team, I would be glad to connect.
