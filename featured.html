Independent Initiative · 2026–Present

Lighthouse Curation

Founder

An evolving practice for the thoughtful integration and curation of diverse forms of existing information to support clearer observation, transparent understanding, and more informed decision-making.

Developed through Lighthouse Curation, my independent research and publishing initiative, this work explores reusable approaches for organizing and connecting spatial, documentary, quantitative, and community-derived information. 02

County of San Diego · 2020–2023

Agricultural Water Quality Program Site Prioritization System

Developed through my County of San Diego work, the Threat to Water Quality site assessment and prioritization framework connected environmental program operations, data management, and decision support. It integrated watershed priorities across nine watersheds, Water Quality Improvement Plan information, inspection data, environmental risk factors, and operational considerations.

The framework supported defensible identification and onboarding of higher-priority agricultural operations, strategic allocation of inspection resources, workload balancing, annual regulatory reporting, and long-term program planning.

I designed and managed the framework while coordinating across departments, technical staff, and regulatory stakeholders. The work supported expansion of the regulated inventory from roughly 300 to more than 1,600 sites and an inspection team that grew from two to six inspectors.

03

Cross-departmental County initiative · 2023–2025

Water Ways Report

This regional water resilience initiative brought together environmental reporting, watershed conditions, public input, and planning related to water conservation, recycling, storage, stormwater capture, and resilience strategies.

I managed technical consultants, stakeholder engagement, and cross-departmental workflows; synthesized technical analysis and stakeholder perspectives; and led final report development, review coordination, feedback integration, and finalization. The resulting report was provided to the San Diego County Board of Supervisors in April 2025.

04

Countywide food systems initiative · 2024–2026

Food Justice Community Action Plan

This collaborative County initiative supported equitable access to healthy food through community-informed planning, cross-departmental coordination, and actionable recommendations related to food access, public health, local agriculture, and sustainability.

I coordinated stakeholder engagement, led seven community meetings, facilitated interdepartmental collaboration, and analyzed qualitative and community-derived information. The team synthesized more than 1,500 public comments into over 200 community ideas and 20 potential County actions for a plan presented to the County Board of Supervisors on December 11, 2024.
