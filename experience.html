Independent Initiative

Lighthouse Curation

Founder

I founded Lighthouse Curation as an evolving practice and personal initiative focused on integrating environmental information, geospatial technologies, data systems, and public knowledge to support clearer observation and more informed decision-making.

It provides a structure for developing environmental observatories, technical prototypes, reports, publications, and open projects that connect my experience in environmental programs with my growing work in data engineering and geospatial systems.

Lighthouse Curation complements my professional career by giving me a place to explore ideas, continue learning, and build useful work across disciplines. My role is that of founder and builder: shaping the framework, developing the projects, and publishing the resulting work.
